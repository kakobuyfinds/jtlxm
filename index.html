From discovery to a considered order

How to use a CSSBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet organizes research. It does not reserve stock, verify authenticity or calculate your final delivered price. Keep the product listing and your selected variation together throughout the process.

STEP 01 / FIND

Open the exact product

Browse the directory and open a CAFANS detail page. Look for the original seller link and available agent options. A category page or catalog homepage is not a seller item URL.

STEP 02 / CHECK

Confirm the order details

If ordering with CSSBuy, use its official account interface and confirm that it accepts the source link and product. Review size, color, quantity, domestic delivery and the full payment breakdown before paying.

STEP 03 / REVIEW

Review before shipping

When warehouse photos are available, compare the item with your saved variation and measurements. Resolve discrepancies before arranging international delivery. Check the current return terms and shipping quote.

A more useful comparison

Look beyond the item price

Two finds with similar prices can have different delivered costs. Use one currency and record each charge separately. Enter your own figures in the worksheet; blank fields are unknown, not free.

  • Fit: compare garment measurements with a piece you already own. Keep chest width separate from chest circumference.
  • Listing: save the original URL, size, color, quantity and date. Check whether the price refers to a single item or a set.
  • Photos: compare color, visible construction, included parts and requested measurements. Photos cannot establish authenticity or hidden durability.
  • Shipping: request a quote for your destination and actual parcel details. Dimensions can affect billable weight.
  • Terms: confirm return eligibility and which charges can be refunded before you commit.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product-research list used to organize finds, source links, prices and notes before ordering with a shopping agent. This page provides a browsable CAFANS selection and buying checks rather than an official CSSBuy document.

Where do the CSSBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons go?

Both buttons open https://cafans.com. Product cards open individual CAFANS listings. This page does not process payment or place orders on your behalf.

Are these real products from CAFANS?

The displayed names, photographs, listing URLs and USD reference prices were imported from the CAFANS homepage on November 19, 2026. This is a dated selection, not an automatically synchronized copy of the entire catalog. Recheck each listing for current details.

Does every item work with CSSBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified for every item. Open the listing, locate its original seller URL and check acceptance in your CSSBuy account. Confirm the product type, available variation and delivery options before making a payment.

Do the listed prices include international shipping?

These are item-price estimates shown by CAFANS. They are not delivered totals. Domestic freight, agent services, payment charges, international delivery and any applicable taxes or handling charges need separate confirmation.

Are the images warehouse QC photos?

The images shown here are catalog images. Do not treat them as inspection evidence for a specific order. Review the warehouse photographs associated with your own order and request additional measurements or views when necessary.

Can I download the selection as a spreadsheet?

With JavaScript enabled, use “Export filtered CSV” below the directory. The export includes the currently visible product names, categories, reference prices, CAFANS URLs and source-check date. It does not contain verified original seller URLs or live inventory.

Is this website affiliated with CSSBuy?

This is an independent discovery page. CSSBuy and other names mentioned belong to their respective owners. Product names reproduce catalog labels and do not establish authenticity, authorization or an endorsement.
